Onboarding

Everything for bringing a new client on board.

The step-by-step process for onboarding a new client. Follow each step in order. Where a template exists, open it, copy it, fill in the [brackets], and send.

1Send the Welcome Message

Once a client agrees to start (payment confirmed), send this message via WhatsApp.

Hey [Name], welcome to Nest Creativs! Excited to start working on your content.

Here's how this works: you're on the 8 videos/month plan (short-form edits + thumbnails), with 2 rounds of revisions per video. No contracts, no lock-in, you can pause or cancel anytime, we just ask for a heads up before the next cycle starts.

Two quick things to kick things off:

1. Fill out this short form so we understand your brand, audience, and style: https://forms.gle/JdDQ3ojTrMXZEvxM7
2. Drop the link of the raw footage here in the chat.

Send footage over whenever you shoot it, we'll batch and edit as it comes in rather than waiting for everything at once. Let's get started!
2Send the Kickoff Form

Share the kickoff form link (same link as in the welcome message, or send separately if the client asks for it again). The form collects: contact info, brand and positioning, target audience, content style and references, footage and logistics, and non-negotiables including any brokerage compliance requirements.

Mark "Kickoff form sent" as done in the Entry panel once sent. 

https://forms.gle/JdDQ3ojTrMXZEvxM7
3Confirm Payment ReceivedGate

Do not proceed past this step until payment has landed. Log the amount and date. This is the actual gate before any onboarding work begins. A client should not move to Active status without this confirmed.

4Set Up the Footage Folder

Create a dedicated Frame.io folder for the client (naming convention: client name). Share the folder link with the client once the edited batch is ready.

5Begin Editing

Hand off to the regular production and delivery workflow (tracked separately in Excel; video counts, revisions, and payment cycles are not tracked in this tool).
